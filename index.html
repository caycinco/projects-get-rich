Arena (paper)

Three code-level paper traders ($100 each) compete on newly listed Solana pairs from DexScreener. Every entry passes the desk rug filter and the desk Risk Manager (1% risk/trade, -6% stop, +12% trailing, 2% daily loss, 8% max drawdown, 3 positions, $20k min liquidity). Hard exit at 2x entry. Every 24h, if each trader has 20+ closed trades, every trader with net P&L ≤ 0 is retired (or the worst by risk-adjusted return if all are profitable) and replaced by a mutated one. A trader halted by its loss breaker is retired at the daily review even with fewer than 20 trades. Each entry is first argued by a Researcher and a Skeptic; a Lead decides GO or NO-GO.

Controls live on the private control page (owner sign-in required).
